## 🛡️ The Anti-AI-Flagging Visual System in Canva Pro

Pinterest's 2026 computer vision neural networks actively down-rank synthetic AI art, fake 3D characters, and plastic human faces. To guarantee your pins maintain viral organic reach and drive massive outbound clicks:
1. **Zero Synthetic Humans:** Never use Midjourney or DALL-E generated human portraits. Use authentic dark-mode workspace photography, mechanical keyboards, Apple Silicon setups, or clean vector bento boxes.
2. **Bold Swiss Typography:** AI cannot render high-precision vector fonts. Use **Montserrat Black**, **Archivo**, or **Inter Bold** (95pt to 130pt) with `-20` letter-spacing and high contrast.
3. **Structured Bento Grids:** Divide the `1000 × 1500 px` canvas into 3 to 4 rounded cards (`#1E293B` on `#0B0F19`) with subtle `1px solid #334155` borders.
4. **The 5-Second C2PA Metadata Wipe:** If you download any design from Canva that used Magic Studio features, open the image in Windows and hit `Win + Shift + S` to take a 1:1 pixel crop. This instantly wipes all invisible C2PA AI provenance flags.
