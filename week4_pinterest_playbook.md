## 🛡️ The Anti-AI-Flagging Visual System in Canva Pro

To guarantee Pinterest's automated computer vision neural networks and C2PA metadata filters **never flag or de-rank your pins**:
1. **Zero Synthetic Faces or Hands:** Never use AI-generated humanoid portraits with plastic skin or uncanny finger geometry. Use Canva's licensed stock library of authentic workspaces, mechanical keyboards, Apple Silicon laptops, or terminal screens.
2. **Massive Vector Typography:** AI image generators cannot render clean typography. Anchor every pin with massive, ultra-bold headers in **Montserrat Black**, **Archivo**, or **Inter Bold** (90pt to 130pt) with tight letter-spacing (-20 tracking).
3. **Dark-Mode Bento Grids:** Divide the `1000 × 1500 px` canvas into 3 or 4 rounded cards (`#1E293B` on a `#0B0F19` canvas) with subtle `1px solid #334155` borders.
4. **The 5-Second C2PA Metadata Wipe:** If you download any design from Canva that used Magic Studio or background removers, open the PNG on your computer and press `Win + Shift + S` (Windows Snipping Tool) to take a clean crop. This creates a pure raw-pixel image with **zero invisible C2PA provenance tags**.
